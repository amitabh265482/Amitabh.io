FAQ

Common questions from firms.

Which states do you cover?
Washington, Oregon, California and Idaho, with the deepest hands-on experience in WA, OR and CA. Happy to discuss other jurisdictions on request.
Are your rates negotiable?
Yes. Per-file and hourly rates listed here are starting points — volume, retainer arrangements and long-term working relationships are all open to negotiation.
What do you need from our firm to start?
Medical records, billing, any liability documentation and your firm's demand template/preferences (if you have one). I'll ask clarifying questions if anything's missing.
What's the typical turnaround?
24 hours for Short Demands, 24 hours for Time-Limited/Policy-Limit demands, and 24–36 hours for Comprehensive packages, depending on record volume.
Do you work under attorney supervision?
Yes. All work is delivered as litigation-support/drafting services intended for review and use under the supervising attorney's judgment.
