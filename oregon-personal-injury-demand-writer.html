What I account for in a Oregon demand.

Filing deadline
Two years for most personal injury claims (ORS 12.110).
Fault rule
Modified comparative negligence (ORS 31.600): recovery is barred if the claimant's fault is greater than the combined fault of the defendants, so the liability narrative matters more than in a pure-comparative state.
Minimum auto limits
$25,000 / $50,000 / $20,000, with mandatory personal injury protection of $15,000 and uninsured motorist coverage.
PIP
Because every Oregon policy carries PIP, the damages section tracks what PIP has paid and how reimbursement affects the net claim.
Government claims
The Oregon Tort Claims Act requires notice within 180 days of the injury (ORS 30.275).
Authority usually cited
Oregon vehicle code provisions for the collision and uniform civil jury instructions on negligence and damages.

Current as of October 2026. Statutes and limits change, and the supervising attorney confirms every deadline and citation before a demand is sent.
