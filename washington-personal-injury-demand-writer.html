What I account for in a Washington demand.

Filing deadline
Three years for most personal injury claims (RCW 4.16.080).
Fault rule
Pure comparative fault (RCW 4.22.005). The liability section answers contributory-fault arguments before the adjuster raises them.
Minimum auto limits
$25,000 / $50,000 / $10,000. PIP payments and any UIM coverage are accounted for in the damages presentation.
Insurer conduct
Where a carrier delays or undervalues a first-party claim, the demand is written with the Insurance Fair Conduct Act (RCW 48.30.015) and the claims-handling regulations in view.
Authority usually cited
Rules-of-the-road statutes such as following too closely (RCW 46.61.145), Washington pattern jury instructions, and case law that a following driver is presumptively at fault in a rear-end collision.
Government claims
Claims against cities, counties and the state need the statutory tort claim form and a 60-day waiting period before suit (RCW 4.96.020, RCW 4.92.100).

Current as of October 2026. Statutes and limits change, and the supervising attorney confirms every deadline and citation before a demand is sent.
