Exhibit B — Scope of Work

A complete demand-production workflow.

Designed primarily for U.S. personal injury attorneys, firms and litigation teams.

01 / DEMANDS

Comprehensive Demand Packages

Full narrative, facts & liability, statutory authority, injuries & treatment, economic/non-economic damages, future care, loss of income and exhibits.

02 / POLICY

Time-Limited & Policy-Limit Demands

Deadline-sensitive settlement demands, policy-limit framing and complex coverage/umbrella-limit analysis support.

03 / MEDICAL

Medical Record Summaries

Provider-by-provider chronology, diagnoses, ICD-10 codes, procedures, imaging, referrals and treatment progression.

04 / DAMAGES

Damages & Billing Analysis

Past medical expenses, future medical expense tables, loss-of-income calculations and pain-and-suffering presentation.

05 / RESEARCH

Legal Research

California, Washington, Oregon and Idaho statutory/case research using LexisNexis and Westlaw, with AI-assisted research workflows.

06 / QA

Demand Quality Control

Names, dates, pronouns, calculations, medical consistency, exhibit references, citations and final attorney-ready review.

Which states do you cover?
California, Washington, Oregon and Idaho, with the deepest hands-on experience in CA, WA and OR. Happy to discuss other jurisdictions on request.
Are your rates negotiable?
Yes. Per-file and hourly rates listed here are starting points — volume, retainer arrangements and long-term working relationships are all open to negotiation.
What do you need from our firm to start?
Medical records, billing, any liability documentation and your firm's demand template/preferences (if you have one). I'll ask clarifying questions if anything's missing.
