Is there a free trial?

Yes. Firms new to working with me can send their first two cases free of charge. You receive two attorney-ready demands, each within the typical 12–16 hours, with no commitment to continue.

Which states do you cover?

California, Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Texas, Georgia, Alaska and Utah, with the deepest hands-on experience in CA, WA and OR. Happy to discuss other jurisdictions on request.

Are your rates negotiable?

Yes. Per-case and hourly rates listed here are starting points — volume, retainer arrangements and long-term working relationships are all open to negotiation.

Is there a limit on case volume or file size?

No. There is no cap on the number of cases you can send, and large files are welcome — I have handled single cases with 40,000 pages of records. Rates stay negotiable for volume, and every demand is built for accuracy, quality, quantity and precision.

What do you need from our firm to start?

Medical records, billing, any liability documentation and your firm's demand template/preferences (if you have one). I'll ask clarifying questions if anything's missing.

What's the typical turnaround?

Most demands are delivered within 12–16 hours. For bulk or firm-volume orders I can deliver up to 6–10 demands per day, depending on record volume.

Do you work under attorney supervision?

Yes. All work is delivered as litigation-support/drafting services intended for review and use under the supervising attorney's judgment.
