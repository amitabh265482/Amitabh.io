Time-limited and policy-limit demand writing.

A time-limited demand works only if it meets the rules of the state it is sent in. I draft deadline-sensitive settlement demands that set a clear response window and put the carrier on notice of its duty to settle.

What is included.

  • A firm response deadline, stated to the state's minimum period
  • The offer of a full release within policy limits
  • Liability built from the physical evidence and the governing statutes
  • Economic and non-economic damages, itemized and totaled
  • The insurer's good-faith obligation, with supporting authority
  • Multi-layer and umbrella coverage analysis where limits stack

How it works.

I have drafted policy-limit and time-limited demands on coverage from $25,000 to $10 million. The structure follows the state: Code Civ. Proc. § 999 in California, O.C.G.A. § 9-11-67.1 in Georgia, the Stowers doctrine in Texas. Typical turnaround is 12–16 hours.
