What I account for in a Utah demand.

Filing deadline
Four years for most negligence claims (Utah Code § 78B-2-307), longer than in most states.
Fault rule
Modified comparative fault with a 50% bar (Utah Code § 78B-5-818).
Minimum auto limits
$30,000 / $65,000 / $25,000 since 2025, with personal injury protection of at least $3,000.
No-fault threshold
Utah is a no-fault state. A claim for general damages needs more than $3,000 in medical expenses or one of the injuries listed in Utah Code § 31A-22-309, so the demand shows on its face that the threshold is met.
Government claims
The Governmental Immunity Act requires a notice of claim within one year (Utah Code § 63G-7-402).
Authority usually cited
Utah traffic code provisions for the collision and the Model Utah Jury Instructions on negligence and damages.

Current as of October 2026. Statutes and limits change, and the supervising attorney confirms every deadline and citation before a demand is sent.
